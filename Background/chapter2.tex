\section{$\infty$-cosmos}
\label{sec:infct}
The definition of an infinity-cosmos was introduced by Riehl and Verity over the course of a series of papers. That definition has evolved over time. We start by reviewing the current form of the definition from [RV-elements] first, as it is the simplest definition. But then we also mention a more general older definition, which will be the one that is relevant to the project here.

But the way that I would actually write it would be out of order: write the definitions first and then figure out what needs to be explained and then write that before the definitions in the paper.
